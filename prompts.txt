user will upload an invoice to s3, this will trigger validation lambda where the invoice image is ocr-ed.

OCR confidence drives UX only, not whether a human approves:
- If OCR confidence is HIGH (at/above configured threshold): extraction is trusted — verification is automatic (no manual re-entry). User still lands on an approval page and must explicitly approve or reject (never auto-approve from OCR alone).
- If OCR confidence is LOW (below threshold): user lands on a manual verification page — correct/re-enter extracted values, then approve or reject.

Notify reviewer via email with link to the SPA (React). DynamoDB holds workflow state.

On rejection, relevant users get email with why the invoice was rejected.

upon approval or rejection, an analytics lambda will keep track of approved and rejected counts and further analytics which are kept in another stack and be triggered via eventbridge.
